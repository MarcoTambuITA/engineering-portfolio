## The Vision

Engineering curricula excel at teaching technical problem-solving, but bridging the gap between academic lab work and a high-impact engineering career requires deliberate infrastructure. 

As the Professional Development Chair for IEEE at USF, my objective is to transition our branch from a traditional student club into a highly integrated professional launchpad. Rather than managing isolated networking events, I am designing a centralized system that aligns our members' technical project work with direct opportunities in industry, undergraduate research, and venture commercialization.

---

## Strategic Roadmap & Objectives

To maximize regional impact and recruiter ROI, my leadership framework focuses on four core operational pillars:

### 1. The Cross-Club Coalition Network
- **The Strategy:** Spearheading the integration of a unified communication network connecting the Professional Development chairs of USF’s premier engineering societies (IEEE, SHPE, SWE, PES).
- **The Impact:** Eliminating organizational silos to coordinate co-promoted corporate events, scaling student outreach, and optimizing logistical overhead across organizations.

### 2. Targeted Technical Career Programming
- **The Strategy:** Coordinating targeted industry panels, resume teardowns, and an upcoming technical career fair segmented by specific engineering paths (Power & Energy, RF/Wireless, Embedded Systems, and Automation).
- **The Impact:** Providing corporate partners with direct, friction-free access to high-caliber students pre-sorted by technical focus area.

### 3. Student–Faculty Research Pipeline
- **The Strategy:** Building a live, centralized repository on the IEEE USF platform mapping active undergraduate research opportunities, faculty office hours, and specific skill prerequisites.
- **The Impact:** Actively routing qualified student hardware engineers into university labs and leveraging dedicated funding avenues like the Florida High Tech Corridor Undergraduate Research Initiative.

### 4. Hardware Commercialization & Venture Support
- **The Strategy:** Partnering with the Nault Center for Entrepreneurship, USF Connect, and the USF Patent Office to establish specialized workshops on intellectual property, patent filing for personal projects, and early-stage pitching.
- **The Impact:** Equipping student makers with the resources needed to scale their physical prototypes from prototype breadboards into viable tech startups.

---

## Why I Stepped Into This Role

I ran for the Executive Board because I noticed a disconnect: our members are building incredible, complex hardware—from custom Altium PCB layouts to embedded firmware—yet lacked a robust system to showcase that technical depth directly to the people hiring. 

Engineering is inherently collaborative. By treating professional development with the same rigor we apply to hardware debugging, we are building a sustainable pipeline where student technical excellence compounds into elite career placements.
