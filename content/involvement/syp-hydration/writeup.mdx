## The Engineering Problem

syp's device clips onto any water bottle and measures fluid intake in real time. The data feeds into an AI-powered dashboard used by coaches and athletic trainers to monitor hydration at the individual athlete level.

The problem: during games, all team bottles sit in a shared rack. Athletes grab any bottle at halftime. The system had no way to know *which athlete* was drinking from *which bottle* — making individual hydration tracking impossible in practice.


![SYP Device](/content/involvement/syp-hydration/images/SYP.png)

---

## The Solution

Our team engineered a seamless, contact-triggered attribution architecture that automatically links an athlete to their specific bottle the moment it is handled. Rather than forcing a change in user behavior, the approach utilizes a low-latency proximity handshake that validates the user and locks the data stream for the duration of the hydration event.

By operating entirely in the background, this identification framework successfully resolved the shared-rack problem and is now a core component of syp's patent-pending platform.
