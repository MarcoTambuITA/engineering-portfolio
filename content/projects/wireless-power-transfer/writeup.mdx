# Wireless Power Transfer (WPT) Simulation

## The Goal
Magnetic resonant coupling is a powerful technique for transferring energy over mid-range distances without wires. To design an efficient WPT system, I needed to precisely calculate mutual inductance and optimize coil geometry before building physical prototypes.

## Implementation
I developed a comprehensive Python simulation framework capable of modeling multi-coil systems.

### Key Features
- **Biot-Savart Integration**: Calculates magnetic field distribution in 3D space.
- **Mutual Inductance Matrix**: Computes coupling coefficients between arbitrary coil pairs.
- **Efficiency Optimization**: Sweeps through resonant frequencies to find the maximum power transfer efficiency point.

## Results
The simulation allowed me to predict the system's performance with a high degree of accuracy. When I built the physical coils, the measured efficiency was within 5% of the simulated values.

| Parameter | Simulated | Measured |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Resonant Frequency | 1.25 MHz | 1.22 MHz |
| Max Efficiency | 82.4% | 78.1% |
| Q-Factor | 150 | 135 |

The tool proved invaluable for reducing the number of physical iterations needed to achieve the target performance.
